# Mission 3 Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three platforms, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. These technologies are becoming increasingly important for modern applications and organizations.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three providers offer virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, databases, security services, monitoring, and managed Kubernetes platforms. They also use similar cloud concepts such as regions, zones, scalability, virtualization, automation, and pay-as-you-use pricing. However, each provider has different strengths and focuses.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory would benefit from Azure because of its strong Microsoft integration. An AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI, ML, data analytics, and Kubernetes capabilities. A startup may prioritize scalability, flexibility, available services, and cost management when selecting a provider.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that cloud decisions should be based on the customer's actual requirements rather than simply choosing the most popular provider. I learned that factors such as budget, performance, security, scalability, availability, existing infrastructure, technical skills, and future business growth must be considered.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved after completing this mission. I added organized research documents about AWS, Azure, and GCP, comparison tables, client recommendations, a multi-cloud decision matrix, Linux investigation results, and screenshots as evidence. This mission helped me improve my technical documentation skills and gave me a better understanding of how cloud professionals evaluate technologies and make recommendations based on business needs.
